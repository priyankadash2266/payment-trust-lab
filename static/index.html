INTAKE CONTROLS

Keep allegations and outcomes separate

01
Explicit payment selection

A member can have multiple legitimate bill payments. The selected payment must match the supplied member.

02
No automatic fraud finding

Opening a case creates a member allegation; analyst dispositions are stored separately.

03
No live account access

IDs and payments here are simulated. Real financial systems require identity verification and authorization.
